The Ranch

I spent six amazing months working on a horse ranch nestled in the Grampian Mountains of Victoria, Australia. I taught people how to ride horses and led pack rides through the mountain trails.

Visited the 12 Apostles which are a series of limestone stacks located off the coast of Port Campbell National Park, along the Great Ocean Road.

I celebrated Australia Day with friends, enjoying a classic Aussie barbecue, playing backyard cricket, and soaking up the summer sun. Australia Day, held on January 26th, marks the arrival of the First Fleet in 1788 and the beginning of British settlement. While it's a day of national pride for many, it's also a time to reflect on the country’s complex history and acknowledge the experiences of Indigenous Australians.
