Oahu

Spending nine months in Hawaii was like living in a dream—waking up to ocean breezes, volcanic landscapes, and sunsets that felt unreal. I soaked up island life, learned the true meaning of aloha!

The hiking in Hawaii is pure magic! Lush rainforests, volcanic ridges, and ocean views all in one trail. From misty sunrise climbs to dramatic coastal paths, every hike feels like an adventure. During my time here I hiked as many trails as I could and enjoyed every minute.
